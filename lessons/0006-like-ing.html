练习 0006　like doing 和 good at doing

满分 20 分　建议 25 分钟　闭卷（先看下方提示，开始作答后不要翻对照表）

姓名 日期 得分
考前提示（作答时请不要再看） 主练：like / likes / don't like / doesn't like / feel like + -ing。不要写成 like play、likes sit。 be good at + -ing：good at swimming（不要 good at swims）。 书桌前：sitting at / in front of the desk。 已稳回看：he / she / it 加 -s；否定用 doesn't + 原形。 新结构（只在选择题）：It is + 形容词 + to do。这里的 It is 不是天气。
like / feel likelike playing　feel like doing　（不要 like play / feel like to do）
be good atgood at swimming　（不要 good at swim / swims）
he / she / itgoes　doesn't + 原形
It isIt is important to study　It's best for you to get up
